# Booker Peynorth

[All recruits](README.md)

> Sorsley Ende's most trusted retainer. His duties are many, so he does everything twice.

**Classes:** Poisoner → Venomist (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Master Poisoner (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**At Your Service** (3 TP, range 0-4, point): Grant an ally the chance to act twice on their next turn.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
