# Plinius Naveste

[All recruits](README.md)

> A researcher at Hyzante's Ministry of Medicine who would rather study what he likes. Ice in his hands is medicine.

**Classes:** Cryomancer → Frost Scholar (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Ministry Savant (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Cryostasis** (3 TP, range 0-4, point): Freeze an ally solid: fully restore their HP and make them invincible, but they cannot act for 1 turn.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
