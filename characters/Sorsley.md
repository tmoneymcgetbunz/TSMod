# Sorsley Ende

[All recruits](README.md)

> The Saintly Seven's Minister of Salt. He holds his ground, profits from standing still, and keeps a ledger no one else may read.

**Classes:** Shortsword → Dagger Fighter (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Minister of Salt (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Salt Tax** (4 TP, range 0-4, circle 2): Deal non-elemental magic damage to all enemies within range. Each enemy hit adds gold to a purse dropped on your square.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
