# Patriatte Konsar

[All recruits](README.md)

> Minister and leader of the Royalists. A politician with a bow, who knows which way the wind blows.

**Classes:** Marksman → Royalist Marksman (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Lord Marksman (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Patriatte's Gambit** (4 TP, range 1-4, circle 1): Chance to tempt all enemies within range for 2 turn(s).

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
