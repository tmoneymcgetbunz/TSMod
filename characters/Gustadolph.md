# Gustadolph Aesfrost

[All recruits](README.md)

> Archduke of Aesfrost, who raised his nation by promoting talent over birth. His blade carries killing cold.

**Classes:** Frostruler → Frost Archduke (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Sovereign of Winter (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Fire the Deathsknell** (5 TP, range 3-8, circle 2): Deal heavy fire-type physical damage to all units within range, allies included, and set every square there ablaze.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
