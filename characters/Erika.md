# Erika Aesfrost

[All recruits](README.md)

> Archduke Gustadolph's younger sister. She judges others by their lineage, and her bombs do not discriminate.

**Classes:** Bomber → Flame Bomber (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Grand Bomber (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Smoke Bomb** (3 TP, range 1-4, circle 1): Deal non-elemental magic damage to all enemies within range, and have a chance to blind them for 3 turn(s).

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
