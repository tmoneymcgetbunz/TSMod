# Symon Wolffort

[All recruits](README.md)

> The legendary hero of the Saltiron War. His body is failing, but his blade is not.

**Classes:** Divine Blade → Exalted Blade (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Divine Hero (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Under Conviction's Banner** (4 TP, range 1-3, around 3): Grant 1 TP to all allies within range.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
