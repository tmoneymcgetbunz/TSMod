# Rufus Tyran

[All recruits](README.md)

> "Herosbane", a mercenary loyal only to coin who delights in cutting confident warriors down to size.

**Classes:** Staffwielder → Cudgel Fighter (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Cudgel Lord (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Knock Back ★** (3 TP, range 1-1, point): Deal physical damage to an enemy and knock them back 5 squares.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
