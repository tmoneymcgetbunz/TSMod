# Thalas Aesfrost

[All recruits](README.md)

> Aesfrost's prime minister, the Archduke's younger brother. Where his sister lights the fires, he commands the wind.

**Classes:** Windcaster → Gale Minister (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Prime Minister (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Gale of State** (4 TP, range 1-3, cone): Deal wind-type magic damage in a cone. Fire and ice in its path are blown 2 squares onward.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
