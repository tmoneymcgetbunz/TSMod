# Idore Delmira

[All recruits](README.md)

> Hyzante's high priest and the hand behind the hierophant. He means to rise above mortals, and his spells answer him.

**Classes:** Priest → High Priest (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Demigod (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Puppet Master ★** (3 TP, range 1-4, point): Summon the hierophant on a selected square.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
