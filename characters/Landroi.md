# Landroi Falkes

[All recruits](README.md)

> Lord of House Falkes, whose bow-arm surpasses even Symon's. Loyal to the crown to the very last.

**Classes:** Archer → Falkes Archer (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Bow Lord (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Heroic End** (0 TP, range self, point): Fall in battle. Every ally gains strength, magic, defense, magic defense, accuracy, evasion, luck and speed for 2 turns.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
