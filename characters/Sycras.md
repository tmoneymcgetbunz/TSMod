# Sycras Diutte

[All recruits](README.md)

> The honest head of the constabulary. He rallies allies, and keeps the peace by detaining anyone who threatens it.

**Classes:** Staffwielder → Cudgel Fighter (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Cudgel Master (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Crackdown** (4 TP, range 0-2, around 2): Chance to paralyze all enemies within range for 1 turn.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
