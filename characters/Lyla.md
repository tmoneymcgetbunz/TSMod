# Lyla Viscraft

[All recruits](README.md)

> The Great Caster of the Ministry of Medicine, who built the automaton and the Aelfric crystals. Quahaug's mother.

**Classes:** Great Caster → Chrono Caster (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Overseer (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Stop Time** (3 TP, range 1-99, exists_unit 99): Stop everyone but yourself for 2 turn(s), preventing them from taking any actions. Then gain the use of 1 more command. However, you can only move once.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
