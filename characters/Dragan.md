# Dragan Aesfrost

[All recruits](README.md)

> A young genius of the Archives who discovered how to make explosives, out to restore his family's honor.

**Classes:** Flame Wielder → Ash Scholar (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Ember Sage (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Ignite** (4 TP, range 0-4, circle 1): Deal fire-type magic damage to enemies within range, lower their movement by 1 for 3 turn(s), and set any ground ablaze.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
