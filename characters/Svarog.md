# Svarog Aesfrost

[All recruits](README.md)

> Steward of Twinsgate and brother to the late archduke. His forge-hot hammer and iron will hold the line.

**Classes:** Iron Fighter → Iron Master (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Iron Lord (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Eruption ★** (4 TP, range 1-3, around 3): Deal fire-type physical damage to all enemies within range, and set even nonflammable squares ablaze.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
