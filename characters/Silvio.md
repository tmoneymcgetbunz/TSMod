# Silvio Telliore

[All recruits](README.md)

> Lord of House Telliore and keeper of the reservoir. A famed opportunist who takes whatever is not nailed down.

**Classes:** Artificer → Master Artificer (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Lord of Telliore (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Embezzle** (3 TP, range 1-2, point): Deal physical damage to a single enemy and steal all of their TP.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
