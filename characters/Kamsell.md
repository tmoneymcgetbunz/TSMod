# Kamsell Pharant

[All recruits](README.md)

> The Saintly Seven's Minister of Domestic Affairs. Public order, enforced at the point of a lance.

**Classes:** Master Lancer → Lord Lancer (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Minister of Order (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Summary Judgment** (4 TP, range 1-2, point): Deal heavy lightning-type physical damage to a single enemy, with a chance to paralyze them for 2 turn(s). Effect spreads on water.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
