# Exharme Marcial

[All recruits](README.md)

> A proud Aesfrosti swordsman who wears down foes with his blade and rallies allies with his presence.

**Classes:** Swordsman → Swordfighter (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Swordmaster (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Rouse Troops ★** (1 TP, range 1-99, exists_unit 99): Grant 1 TP to all allies, and raise their strength and movement for 3 turn(s).

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
