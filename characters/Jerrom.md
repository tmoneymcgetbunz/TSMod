# Jerrom Laesmi

[All recruits](README.md)

> Chief of the Rosellan village, respected by his people and ready to rise against Hyzante.

**Classes:** Lancer → Rosellan Lancer (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Lance Master (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Uprising** (2 TP, range 1-1, front3): Deal physical damage to enemies across 3 horizontal squares, and delay their turns.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
