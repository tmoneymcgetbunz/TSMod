# Clarus Brokker

[All recruits](README.md)

> A merchant of the Norzelia Consortium, which trades iron and salt with every nation. He takes no side, and he can buy anything.

**Classes:** Magnate → Trade Magnate (level 10, Medal of Bravery) → Consortium Grandee (level 20, Medal of Valor)

## Weapon skill

**Smuggle** (5 TP, range 1-99, point 1): Swap an ally for a party member who isn't deployed.

Bought as the last node of the rank-3 weapon's upgrade tree.
